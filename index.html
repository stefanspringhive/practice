Companion Care

Companionship can make a meaningful difference in someone's day. Our companion-care services provide friendly social interaction and supportive presence.

  • Conversation and social interaction
  • Recreational activities
  • Accompaniment during appropriate activities
  • Reading and other activities
  • Friendly companionship
  • Assistance with maintaining daily routines

Personal Care Assistance

We provide respectful assistance with appropriate daily personal-care activities while maintaining each client's dignity and privacy.

  • Assistance with bathing
  • Grooming assistance
  • Dressing assistance
  • Toileting assistance
  • Mobility assistance
  • Other agreed-upon non-clinical personal-care support

Homemaking

A clean and organized home can make everyday life easier. Our homemaking support helps clients maintain a comfortable living environment.

  • Light housekeeping
  • Laundry
  • Changing linens
  • Meal preparation
  • Grocery assistance
  • Organization of household areas

Respite Care

Family caregivers give so much of themselves. Respite care provides temporary support so family caregivers can take time for work, appointments, errands, rest, or personal responsibilities.
